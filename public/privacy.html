Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes information processed through Liquid Sports websites, individual games, classroom tools, online leagues, the private content-management system and related services.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on the feature, the individual games may process account identifiers such as an email address, game-save data, classroom or league participation information, coach/program selections and content submitted through authorized administrative tools. The main Liquid Sports public website does not require a separate public-site account.

3. Automatically Collected Information

Hosting and database providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request timestamps, security logs and error data necessary to operate and protect the service.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide sign-in, save games, operate classroom and online-league features, publish authorized CMS content, maintain security, troubleshoot problems and improve the service.

5. Service Providers

Liquid Sports uses third-party infrastructure providers for hosting, databases, file storage and authentication. Those providers process information as necessary to deliver their services under their own contractual and privacy obligations.

6. Advertising

Liquid Sports may display paid advertisements. An advertisement can link to an external site. Advertisers do not receive private game-account information from Liquid Sports merely because an ad is displayed or clicked.

7. Merchandise Store

When you follow a Liquid Sports merchandise link, ordering and checkout occur on an external storefront. Information you provide there is processed by that storefront under its own privacy policy; Liquid Sports does not receive private game-account information merely because you shop for merchandise.

8. Sale of Personal Information

Liquid Sports does not sell personal information.

9. Retention and Security

Information is retained as reasonably necessary for game saves, league operation, classroom administration, security and legal obligations. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children and Classroom Use

Classroom use should be supervised by the responsible school, teacher, parent or guardian as appropriate. Classroom administrators should avoid collecting unnecessary personal information through custom question or league fields.

11. Your Choices

You may stop using the service at any time. Account-specific deletion or correction requests can be sent through the contact method provided on the Liquid Sports site. Some records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance or shared league integrity.

12. External Links

External websites linked from ads, articles or other content have their own privacy practices. Liquid Sports is not responsible for those external practices.

13. Changes

This policy may be updated as the Liquid Sports product and infrastructure change.
