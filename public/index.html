Step-by-Step Instructions

Weekly Management Loop

  1. Navigate to the Hoops Manager tab to begin your weekly management cycle.
  2. Answer the trivia questions first to kick off your week.
  3. Spend your six interactions strategically to recruit new prospects or train players.
  4. Click Simulate Game once you have used up all your interactions for the week.
  5. Select Advance to the Next Week to continue your season.

Player Development & Training

  1. Switch to the Training Tab to oversee long-term athlete progression.
  2. Set your overall training focus to align with your team strategy.
  3. Allocate your interactions to execute focused player training and boost specific attributes.

Offseason Strategy

  1. Progress through the schedule to reach the end-of-year offseason week.
  2. Use your six offseason interactions to convince key players to stay or encourage others to leave.
  3. Scout and acquire fresh talent directly from the Recruit or Transfer Station.
